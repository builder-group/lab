marble-instruments.sf2 is a trimmed derivative of GeneralUser GS v2.0.3
by S. Christian Collins.
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php
Source: https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS

Only the melodic bank 0 presets for General MIDI programs 0, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12,
13, 14, 24, 46, 108, 112 and 114 are kept, together with the instruments and
samples they use. No sample data was altered. The file is rebuilt with
`pnpm soundbank:build` (scripts/build-soundbank.ts).
See GENERALUSER-LICENSE.txt for the original author's license.

Audio synthesis uses SpessaSynth core, Apache-2.0, by Spessasus.
https://github.com/spessasus/spessasynth_core
See SPESSASYNTH-LICENSE.txt for its full license.

SpessaSynth's bundled dependencies:
fflate v0.8.3, MIT. See FFLATE-LICENSE.txt.
stb-vorbis v0.0.6, Apache-2.0. See STB-VORBIS-LICENSE.txt.
